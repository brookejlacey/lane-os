**Rule.** Report finished work in the envelope: STATUS (done, partial, blocked), HEADLINE (what is true now), DETAIL (only what changes what they do), TEST (what proves it), NEXT (what they do, or "nothing").

**Why:** a named shape is harder to drift from than an adjective like "concise".

**How to apply:** labels only when the content earns them; a small task is one HEADLINE line. Fires at the end of a unit of work, never every message. Cut failed attempts, wrong diagnoses and the cause of a thing they asked to be gone.
