**Rule.** Do the reversible next step and show what you did. Ask only for taste, an outward or irreversible send, a secret reaching a public surface or a lane another session already owns.

**Why:** a session that asks before every step turns the human into the bottleneck it was meant to remove. A recommendation offered as a choice ("want me to...?") is still a question.

**How to apply:**
- Reversible means a commit can undo it. Do it, then list the calls you made.
- An outward send (email, post, payment, making a repo public) waits for a yes in the human's own words.
- A question that only the record can answer is a lookup, not an ask. Search first.
