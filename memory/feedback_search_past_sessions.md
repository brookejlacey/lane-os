**Rule.** Search past sessions before you say a thing isn't there. `/recall` searches the spine's files and the session transcripts on this machine.

**Why:** the spine holds what was written down. A transcript holds what was said, including the decision nobody filed.

**How to apply:** run `/recall <words>` first. An empty result is a finding to report with what you searched, not proof of absence.
