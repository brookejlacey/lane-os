metadata:
  type: feedback
---

**Rule.** A reply is ten lines or fewer. Bullets, one fact per line, no section headers for one subject, no table unless the reader picks between the rows. Lifted when they ask for detail.

**Why:** most misses measured across a session's replies were reporting failures, not doing failures: the answer was right but buried.

**How to apply:** answer first, then delete every line that does not change what the reader does next. Never render one fact twice. Enforced by the reply-length pair, `docs/reply-length-gate.md`.
