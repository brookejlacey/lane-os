# brain/archive

Where history goes when it leaves a budgeted file. Verbatim, never summarized away:

- `concerns-resolved.md`: closed concerns WITH the evidence that closed them (the probe
  result, the transcript line, the record). A concern is moved here by a human after a
  probe flags it READY TO CLOSE, never by the probe itself.
- `weekly-log-<year>.md`: weeks that aged out of the two-week window in `WEEKLY_LOG.md`.
- Anything pruned from `ACTIVE_NOW.md` that is worth keeping.

Nothing here is loaded at session start, so nothing here is budgeted.
